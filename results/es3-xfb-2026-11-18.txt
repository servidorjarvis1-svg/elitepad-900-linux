## Known limits
- The capture is copied into the buffer's CPU copy after each draw that captures, with the GPU
  idle first: correct, slow for heavy transform feedback use.
- PRIMITIVES_GENERATED counts only draws with a capture bound.
- Built-in legacy varyings (gl_TexCoord, gl_FrontColor) captured from a desktop GL shader:
  remap_legacy_varyings moves them, the capture table doesn't follow (ES has none).
- Next: dEQP-GLES3 (transform_feedback.*), Chromium 150 without the override.
